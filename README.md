# Blood and Ice

An original dark-fantasy trilogy.

**Book One — "Wild Boy"** follows a feral, enslaved boy wrecked alone on a tropical
death-jungle that kills every human but him.

> **[→ Start reading Book One](BOOK-ONE.md)** — linked table of contents, 30 chapters, 73,718 words.

---

A boy who has only ever been property wakes alone on **Saqarro** — heat, rot, fever,
venom, and monster-peoples who call it home. Humans who land here die. He does not.

Taken in by the Vorth, he rises from stray to king of the wild things and finds the
first family he has ever known. Then his own kind comes across the water — slavers,
chains, a blood-trade — and takes everything.

At the bottom of the pit, the old blood-force in his veins stirs for the first time.
**The Red.** And across the water, something colder is already coming.

---

**The trilogy:**

1. **Wild Boy** *(this book — draft complete)* — survival, belonging, and the trap
2. **The Red and the Bled** — the rise from the bottom of a blood-economy
3. **The Cold Comes** — the Bloodless arrive; the blood-tithe fails

The "Ice" of the title is the **Cold** — the bloodless thing that hunts his line —
not a climate. It is seeded in the prologue and pays off across all three books.
