# Blood and Ice Outline

## Full Arc

### Act I: Property In The Green

Rook escapes one form of ownership into Saqarro, a world designed to kill humans. His wrong blood keeps him alive before he understands that survival itself is evidence. He learns water, heat, rot, venom, fire, and fear as first laws.

### Act II: The People With Teeth

The Vorth take him in. Kinship is not softness: it is food, rank, command, threat, and keeping. Rook learns language, usefulness, shame, play, rivalry, and the first real answer to the second hunger.

### Act III: King Of Wild Things

Rook proves himself through hunt, challenge, mercy, and public consequence. His rise gives him love and enemies at once. Rule is shown as survival made visible, not destiny bestowed.

### Act IV: His Own Kind

Humans return with ships, sickness, water discipline, trade, rope, and the promise of recognition. Rook sees the trap but cannot abandon the people who made him a person.

### Act V: The Pit

Captured and carried across water, Rook becomes property again in a more sophisticated machine. The pit turns ownership into spectacle. At the bottom, with Marrow beside him, the Red stirs but does not save him. Count, ground, wit, wrong-blood, and a kept name give him one handspan of room.
